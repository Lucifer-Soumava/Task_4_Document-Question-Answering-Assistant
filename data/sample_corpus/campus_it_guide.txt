STUDENT IT AND DIGITAL FACILITIES GUIDE

Institution
-----------
Ideal Institute of Engineering (IIE)
Kalyani Shilpanchal, opposite Kalyani Shilpanchal Railway Station
P.O. Kalyani, District Nadia, West Bengal - 741235, India

ABOUT CAMPUS CONNECTIVITY
-------------------------
The official Ideal Institute of Engineering website describes the campus as
having high-speed Wi-Fi connectivity and smart classrooms equipped with
interactive smart boards and LCD projectors.

The public website does not specify the student Wi-Fi network name (SSID),
login username format, password, or a self-service Wi-Fi registration process.
Students should obtain the current connection instructions and credentials
directly from the institute's authorized staff.

Students should keep their college account credentials private and should not
share passwords with other people. Do not send passwords by email or include
them in support requests.

COMPUTER LABORATORIES AND DIGITAL FACILITIES
--------------------------------------------
IIE's official website describes advanced laboratories and technology-enabled
learning facilities on campus. The institute also describes the use of smart
classrooms with interactive smart boards and LCD projectors.

The public website does not publish a confirmed computer-laboratory room
number, student lab access schedule, or specific laboratory opening and
closing hours. Students should confirm lab availability and access rules with
their department or the staff responsible for the laboratory.

REPORTING COMPUTER, NETWORK, OR PROJECTOR PROBLEMS
--------------------------------------------------
For assistance with a campus computer, Wi-Fi connection, classroom projector,
or other technical issue, students should contact the appropriate department
office or institute staff and follow the instructions provided by the
institute.

The institute's public contact page lists the following general contact
channels:

General institute email: enquiry@iiekalyani.com
Telephone: 033-2502-5629 / 033-2502-5630

These are general institute contact details published on the official
website. The website does not identify them as a dedicated IT help-desk
email address or IT support hotline.

When reporting a technical issue, students should describe:
- Their department and class or laboratory.
- The device or service affected (for example, campus Wi-Fi, desktop, or
  classroom projector).
- The approximate time when the problem occurred.
- The error message, if one appeared.

Students must never include their account password in a support request.

PASSWORD AND ACCOUNT SUPPORT
----------------------------
The public IIE website does not describe a specific student account password
reset portal or password-reset procedure.

Students who cannot access a college-provided account should contact the
appropriate institute office or authorized staff member and ask for the
current account recovery procedure. Students should verify that any password
reset link or instruction comes from an official institute source.

Do not share passwords or one-time verification codes with anyone.

STUDENT RESPONSIBILITIES
------------------------
1. Use campus internet and computing facilities for legitimate academic and
   institutional purposes.
2. Keep college account credentials confidential.
3. Log out of shared computers after completing work.
4. Do not install unauthorized software or change laboratory computer
   settings without permission.
5. Report damaged equipment, suspicious messages, or network problems to
   the responsible institute staff.
6. Follow the latest instructions displayed in the laboratory or issued by
   the department.
