IDEAL INSTITUTE OF ENGINEERING (IIE), KALYANI
LIBRARY INFORMATION AND STUDENT GUIDELINES

1. ABOUT THE IIE LIBRARY
------------------------
The library of Ideal Institute of Engineering (IIE), Kalyani supports the
academic and learning needs of students and faculty members.

According to the institute's official library page, the library has separate
reading and lending sections. The page describes a collection of more than
15,000 book volumes covering over 3,000 titles, along with printed magazines,
national and international journals, and newsletters.

The library also provides access to digital information resources, including
J-GATE and DELNET. The institute states that its library is automated and
uses KOHA library management software for online catalogue searching. The
online catalogue facility is described as accessible from within the campus.

2. LIBRARY SERVICES AND RESOURCES
---------------------------------
The IIE library's published facilities include:

- Reading and lending sections.
- Textbooks and other reference materials.
- Printed magazines and national and international journals.
- Newsletters and other academic publications.
- Digital library facilities.
- Access to J-GATE electronic journal resources.
- Access to DELNET library and information resources.
- Online catalogue searching through KOHA library management software.
- Assistance from library staff and the librarian.

Students should use the catalogue and ask library staff for help when
searching for books, journals, or other academic resources.

3. LIBRARY WORKING HOURS
------------------------
The institute's 2021-22 AICTE mandatory disclosure listed library working
hours as 10:00 AM to 5:30 PM.

This timing comes from an older disclosure and may have changed. The current
library webpage does not publish a replacement timetable. Students should
confirm the current opening hours, holidays, and service timings with the
library before visiting.

4. BOOK BORROWING AND RETURNS
-----------------------------
The institute's public library webpage describes separate reading and
lending sections, but it does not publish the current student borrowing
rules in detail.

The following details are not specified on the public library webpage:

- The maximum number of books a student may borrow at one time.
- The borrowing period or due-date rules.
- Whether book renewals are allowed and how many renewals are permitted.
- Whether books can be reserved.
- The overdue fine, if any.
- The replacement charge for lost or damaged books.
- The exact procedure for issuing and returning books.

Students should confirm these rules with the IIE librarian or library staff
before borrowing books. Do not assume that borrowing limits, loan periods,
or fines from another institution apply to IIE.

5. READING ROOM AND LIBRARY CONDUCT
-----------------------------------
Students using the library should:

- Maintain silence and avoid disturbing other readers.
- Handle books, journals, furniture, and library equipment carefully.
- Return materials to the appropriate library staff or designated location.
- Follow instructions given by the librarian and library staff.
- Ask for assistance when they cannot locate a resource.
- Follow the institute's current rules for borrowing, return, and use of
  library facilities.

The public IIE library webpage does not provide a complete list of conduct
rules, so students should follow the notices and instructions displayed
inside the library.

6. DIGITAL LIBRARY AND ONLINE CATALOGUE
---------------------------------------
The IIE library states that it is automated and uses KOHA library management
software for online catalogue searches. Students can use the catalogue to
search for library resources from within the campus.

The library also provides access to J-GATE and DELNET. These services help
students and faculty members discover academic literature, journals, and
other research resources.

For access instructions, login requirements, or assistance with digital
resources, students should contact the librarian or library staff.

7. LOST OR DAMAGED BOOKS
------------------------
The current public library webpage does not specify the exact procedure or
charges for lost or damaged books.

A student who loses or damages a library item should inform the librarian
or library staff as soon as possible and follow the official replacement
or compensation procedure communicated by the institute.

Do not assume a replacement fee or penalty unless it has been confirmed
by the library.

8. IMPORTANT DETAILS TO CONFIRM WITH THE LIBRARY
------------------------------------------------
Students should contact the IIE library to confirm:

- Current library opening and closing hours.
- Student book borrowing limits.
- Book loan duration and renewal policy.
- Reservation facility and procedure.
- Overdue fines and payment procedure.
- Rules for reference books, journals, and periodicals.
- Replacement procedure and charges for lost or damaged items.
- Access procedure for KOHA, J-GATE, DELNET, and digital resources.
- Library holidays and any changes to normal working hours.
